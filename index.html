Revit MEP Full-Discipline Modelling

HVAC

  • Ductwork (supply / return / exhaust) modelled to LOD 300–400
  • AHU, FCU, VAV, chiller & cooling tower placement
  • Pressure drop analysis & duct sizing per ASHRAE 62.1 / SMACNA
  • Full clash detection with structural & plumbing
  • UAE DM / ADDC-compliant layouts for Gulf projects

Plumbing

  • Cold water, hot water, drainage & vent systems
  • Pipe sizing per UPC (USA), BS EN 12056 (UK/EU), UAE local codes
  • Isometric drawings generated directly from Revit model
  • Water hammer analysis integration
  • Greywater & rainwater harvesting layouts for green building projects

Fire Protection

  • Sprinkler layouts per NFPA 13 / FM Global (USA & Gulf)
  • Standpipe & hose system modelling
  • Fire suppression (clean agent, CO₂, foam) for data centres & industrial
  • Compliance with UAE Civil Defence / Saudi SBC / Qatar QCDD
  • Hydraulic calculation references embedded in model

Electrical

  • Lighting, power, ELV, fire alarm & CCTV conduit routing
  • Switchboard, MDB, DB placement & cable tray modelling
  • NEC (USA) / IEC 60364 / UAE DEWA & SEWA compliant
  • Load schedules & panel schedules from Revit
  • BMS / IBMS integration coordination
