Why Join Us

A Place Where Engineers Thrive

We've built a culture of excellence, collaboration, and continuous growth. Here's what makes Central Mechanical a great place to build your career.

Competitive Salary & Bonuses

Industry-leading compensation packages with performance bonuses and annual merit increases tied to professional growth.

Comprehensive Health Insurance

Full medical, dental, and vision coverage for employees and families, with the company covering the majority of premiums.

401(k) with Company Match

Generous 401(k) program with company match up to 5% of salary, helping you build long-term financial security.

Professional Development Budget

$3,000+ annual budget per employee for conferences, certifications, Autodesk training, and professional organization memberships.

Flexible Work Arrangements

Hybrid work options with flexible scheduling for most positions. We trust our team to get great work done while maintaining work-life balance.

Generous Paid Time Off

20+ days of PTO plus 10 paid holidays per year, increasing with tenure. We encourage our team to recharge and come back energized.

Latest Technology & Equipment

High-performance Dell Precision workstations, dual 4K monitors, FARO/Leica scanners, and the latest Autodesk software suite for every engineer.

Collaborative Team Environment

Flat organizational structure, open-door leadership, and a supportive team culture where every voice is valued and ideas are welcomed.

Senior BIM Modeler

MEP Engineer (Mechanical)

  • PE license preferred (EIT considered)

  • Healthcare or data center experience a plus

Scan to BIM Specialist

  • Valid driver's license for field travel

Project Manager — MEP
