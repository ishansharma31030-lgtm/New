Full-Scope MEP

Comprehensive MEP Engineering Services

End-to-end Mechanical, Electrical, and Plumbing engineering for projects of any size or complexity across all building sectors.

What We Offer

Complete MEP Design Solutions

From HVAC load calculations to electrical distribution and plumbing systems, our licensed engineers handle every aspect of MEP design with precision and expertise.

Mechanical Engineering (HVAC)

Our mechanical team designs efficient, code-compliant HVAC systems for all building types. We use HAP and Trane Trace for precise load calculations and energy modeling.

  • HVAC System Design & Sizing
  • Mechanical Load Calculations
  • Equipment Selection & Specifications
  • Ductwork Design & Sizing
  • Energy Modeling & Analysis
  • Chilled Water Systems
  • Boiler & Heating Systems
  • Mechanical Room Layout

Electrical Engineering

Full-scope electrical system design from primary service through branch circuits. Our electrical team is proficient in ETAP for power system analysis and AGi32 for lighting analysis.

  • Power Distribution Systems
  • Lighting Design & Analysis
  • Emergency Power Systems
  • Fire Alarm Systems
  • Generator Sizing & Design
  • Switchgear & Panelboard Design
  • Electrical Load Calculations
  • Low Voltage Systems

Plumbing & Fire Protection

Comprehensive plumbing design covering all water systems, drainage, fire suppression, and specialty systems including medical gas for healthcare facilities.

  • Domestic Water Systems
  • Sanitary & Waste Systems
  • Storm Drainage Design
  • Fire Suppression (Sprinkler)
  • Medical Gas Systems
  • Plumbing Fixture Selection
  • Grease Interceptor Systems
  • Water Heater & Booster Systems
