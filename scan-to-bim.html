Scan to BIM Services

Precision from reality capture — transforming existing buildings into accurate, intelligent BIM models using state-of-the-art laser scanning technology.

What is Scan to BIM?

Reality Capture Meets Digital Engineering

Scan to BIM is the process of capturing the existing conditions of a building or site using 3D laser scanning technology, then converting that high-accuracy point cloud data into an intelligent Building Information Model.

The result is a dimensionally accurate, data-rich BIM model of existing conditions — perfect for renovation planning, facility management, heritage preservation, and as-built documentation of installed MEP systems.

Industry standard accuracy: Our scanning process achieves ±1-3mm scanning accuracy with modeling tolerances of ±5mm, meeting the most demanding project specifications.

Our Process

Scan to BIM Workflow

Our proven 4-step process delivers accurate BIM models that meet your project specifications every time.

1

3D Laser Scanning

On-site scanning using FARO Focus 3D or Leica RTC360 equipment, capturing millions of data points per second with ±1mm accuracy.

2

Point Cloud Processing

Registering, cleaning, and processing scan data in Autodesk ReCap to create a unified, accurate point cloud for modeling.

3

BIM Modeling

Creating an intelligent Revit BIM model from the point cloud reference, adding parametric objects, MEP systems, and project data.

4

Quality Verification

Rigorous QA/QC process validating model accuracy against the point cloud data, ensuring every element meets specified tolerances.

Applications

Where We Apply Scan to BIM

Our Scan to BIM services are used across a wide range of project types where existing conditions must be accurately captured and digitized.

Renovation & Retrofitting Projects

Capturing existing conditions before renovation to plan new MEP systems and avoid conflicts with existing structure.

Heritage Building Preservation

Creating precise digital records of historic buildings for preservation, restoration, and future reference.

Facility Management & Maintenance

Providing accurate as-built BIM models to support ongoing facility operations, maintenance, and space planning.

As-Built Documentation

Generating LOD 500 as-built BIM models by verifying installed conditions against design intent.

Clash Detection for Existing Conditions

Identifying clashes between new design elements and existing building systems before construction begins.
