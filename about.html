Our Story

15+ Years of Engineering Excellence

Founded in 2009 in Houston, Texas, Central Mechanical LLC began as a small team of passionate MEP engineers determined to bring a higher standard of precision and technology to the industry. What started with a handful of engineers has grown into a team of 50+ highly credentialed MEP professionals.

Over 15 years, we have completed 500+ projects spanning healthcare, commercial towers, industrial campuses, data centers, and more — earning a reputation as one of the most trusted MEP engineering firms in the Gulf Coast region and beyond.

Today, we are pioneers in BIM technology, Scan to BIM services, and MEP automation, helping our clients build smarter, faster, and with greater confidence.

Our Journey

Company Milestones

2009

Company Founded

Central Mechanical LLC established in Houston, TX with a mission to transform MEP engineering through technology.

2011

First Major Hospital Project

Completed St. Luke's Medical Center expansion — the project that put Central Mechanical on the healthcare map.

2013

BIM Department Established

Launched dedicated BIM department with full Autodesk Revit suite and Navisworks coordination capabilities.

2015

ISO 9001:2015 Certified

Achieved ISO 9001:2015 quality management certification, formalizing our commitment to process excellence.

2017

Scan to BIM Services

Invested in FARO and Leica laser scanning equipment to launch our reality capture and Scan to BIM division.

2019

10-Year Anniversary

Celebrated 10 years of operation with 300+ completed projects and a team of 35+ professionals.

2021

MEP Automation Division

Launched dedicated MEP automation team focused on Dynamo scripting, Revit API, and digital twin technology.

2023

500+ Projects Milestone

Expanded to 50+ professionals and surpassed 500 completed projects across 12 states nationwide.

Digital Twin Pioneering
